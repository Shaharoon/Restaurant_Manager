1. Introduction

Welcome to Restaurant Manager ("we", "our", or "us"), owned and operated by Haider's Developers. Restaurant Manager is a comprehensive restaurant management, point-of-sale (POS), and order-tracking application designed to help restaurant owners, cafe managers, food outlet staff, and administrators efficiently manage digital menus, table reservations, kitchen order tickets (KOT), customer bill generation, thermal receipt printing, and daily financial ledgers.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal and business information. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how it is used, how it is kept secure, and your rights regarding your data.

2. Information We Collect

A. Restaurant Profile Information

When you create or update an account for your restaurant, cafe, or eatery, we may collect:

  • Restaurant / Business Name and Owner's Full Name
  • Contact Phone / WhatsApp Number
  • Email Address
  • Business Address / City
  • Encrypted credentials (or basic Google profile information if signing in via Google Sign-In)

B. Menu Items, Orders & Transaction Data

To provide core POS, KOT, and restaurant management services, you enter operational food business data into the app, which may include:

  • Food categories, menu items, item prices, and modifier notes
  • Table numbers, dine-in / take-away / delivery order status, and bill details
  • Customer names, contact numbers, and delivery addresses (for order tracking and receipts)
  • Daily cash, card, and digital payment records and sales ledgers

C. Biometric Authentication Data (Fingerprint)

  • If you enable fingerprint or biometric login to restrict administrative or cashier settings, the verification is processed 100% locally on your device by the Android OS BiometricPrompt API.
  • We do NOT collect, transmit, access, or store your biometric data on any server.

D. Device and Technical Information

  • Device model, OS version, Bluetooth status (for thermal receipt printer integration), network connection type, app crash logs, and Android Advertising ID (AAID) used for application stability and advertising services.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your account and manage multi-device access across waiters, kitchen display terminals, and cashiers.
  • Synchronize, backup, and restore your menu, sales data, and billing receipts securely in the cloud.
  • Enable direct printing of receipts and KOTs via Bluetooth or Thermal POS printers.
  • Allow you to send digital e-receipts and order updates to customers via third-party messaging platforms (such as WhatsApp) upon your direct action.
  • Deliver non-personalized or personalized advertisements via Google Mobile Ads.
  • Diagnose technical errors, maintain application stability, and prevent unauthorized account usage.

    • Cloud Firestore / Realtime Database: For instant order status syncing between waiter devices, kitchen screens, and cashier desks.

5. Data Security

All communication between the Restaurant Manager application and our servers is secured using TLS/HTTPS encryption. All sensitive user credentials are cryptographically hashed and protected against unauthorized access.

6. Data Retention and Account / Data Deletion

  • Retention: We maintain your account details, menu items, and transaction logs for as long as your account is active to provide reliable multi-device cloud synchronization and data history.
  • Data Deletion: You retain complete control to request permanent removal of your account and all associated data.
    • To request account and data deletion, please send an email to shaharoonhaider@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - Restaurant Manager", stating your registered email or phone number.
    • Upon identity verification, your account and all related database records will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Restaurant Manager is tailored for business operations and food service professionals aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13.
